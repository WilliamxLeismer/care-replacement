How the model works

01

Replace purchased services

Model the paid childcare, ongoing support, and household duties a surviving household would actually need to buy. Do not price every unpaid hour automatically.

02

Follow ages over time

Ages, school schedules, dependent-specific support, and care arrangements determine each year's cash outflow. Siblings' nanny hours overlap rather than being added together.

03

Calculate today's funding need

Escalate future costs, discount end-of-year cash flows, deduct eligible annual survivor benefits, and subtract insurance or dedicated available assets from the present-value requirement.

Coverage estimate = present value of annual funding shortfalls + one-time costs − existing insurance − dedicated assets

Annual shortfalls cannot go below zero. Benefit excess in one year is not assumed available to fund another year. The suggested policy size is rounded up to the next $50,000. No premium or underwriting estimate is provided.

Sources and limitations

Starting assumptions are illustrative US budgeting inputs, not location-specific quotations. Enter quotes from qualified providers, verify survivor benefits, and consult employment/tax guidance for your jurisdiction.
